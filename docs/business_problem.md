# Business Problem Statement

## 1. Company Background

We are working with a hypothetical e-commerce mobile application company with approximately 500,000 registered customers.

The company allows customers to browse products, place orders, make payments, provide reviews and participate in marketing campaigns.

The company collects information about customer registrations, app sessions, product interactions, transactions, reviews and campaign activities.

## 2. Current Business Problems

The company is facing the following challenges:

### Problem 1: Customer Churn
Customers are becoming inactive or uninstalling the application. The company cannot reliably identify which customers are likely to leave.

### Problem 2: Decreasing Customer Engagement
Customer sessions, product views and interactions are decreasing. The company needs to understand customer engagement patterns.

### Problem 3: Lack of Customer Segmentation
The company treats many customers similarly, even though their purchasing behaviour and preferences differ.

### Problem 4: Low Marketing Conversion
Marketing campaigns are not generating the expected number of purchases. The company needs to identify customers who are more likely to convert.

### Problem 5: Negative Customer Feedback
The company receives customer reviews and complaints but lacks an automated way to identify common issues and sentiment.

### Problem 6: Lack of Personalized Recommendations
Customers may not discover products relevant to their interests and previous purchasing behaviour.

## 3. Business Impact

These problems may contribute to:

- Lost customers and revenue.
- Lower customer retention.
- Inefficient marketing expenditure.
- Poor customer experience.
- Missed cross-selling and upselling opportunities.

These impacts are hypotheses to investigate using data, not established company results.

## 4. Proposed Solution

Develop an AI-powered Business Intelligence and Customer Intelligence Platform using:

- Excel for initial analysis and reporting.
- SQL for data extraction and database analysis.
- Python for data processing and exploratory analysis.
- Machine Learning for customer predictions and segmentation.
- Deep Learning and NLP for advanced text analysis where justified.
- Power BI for interactive business dashboards.
- FastAPI for model integration and deployment.

## 5. Overall Business Goal

Help the company make data-driven decisions by understanding customer behaviour, predicting customer risks and identifying opportunities to improve customer retention, engagement and conversion.

## 6. Project Scope

The project will initially focus on:

1. Customer churn prediction.
2. Customer engagement analysis.
3. Customer segmentation.
4. Marketing conversion prediction.
5. Customer sentiment analysis.
6. Product recommendation.

## 7. Initial Assumptions

- The company is hypothetical.
- The initial customer base is assumed to be 500,000 registered users.

- Historical customer activity and transaction data will be simulated or sourced from a suitable public dataset.

- Business impact will be evaluated using measurable metrics and clearly stated assumptions.
